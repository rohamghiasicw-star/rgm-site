The challenge

Drainage work is bought in a hurry

Nobody researches a drainage company for a fortnight. A basement is taking water, or a line is backing up. The person holding the phone calls one of the first businesses Google shows them. The map block at the top of the page takes most of that attention, and everything under it takes the rest.

Vancouver makes that a harder problem than a compact town does. A profile can look healthy at its own address and fall away completely for somebody searching from a neighbourhood twenty minutes away. That is exactly what the March scan shows. Three drainage companies averaging above them, and the top row of the grid still reading 8 to 12 while points in the south of it read 1 and 2.

The category is crowded too. Franchise operators, plumbing groups and lead brokers all chase the same searches, and most of them outspend an independent.

What we did

The same levers, in the same order

Google Business Profile first. Primary category, secondary categories, services, service area, hours, description. All of it set the way Google reads it, not the way an owner would write it. Photos go up on a schedule. Posts go out on a schedule. The profile gets worked every week instead of being filled in once and left alone.

Service and area pages second. A page for every service the company actually sells, and a page for every neighbourhood it actually covers. Area pages are written from real research into the neighbourhood. Never spun from one template with the name swapped out. Near duplicate pages do not rank and do not convert. Schema and index submission are part of the build, not a later cleanup.

Citations third. Name, address and phone get matched across the directory set so nothing on the open web contradicts the profile. Inconsistent listings are quiet drag and they are cheap to fix once the profile is settled.

Interlinking holds it together. Services link to the areas they are sold in, areas link back to the services they need, and both hang off the locations hub. That is what stops a new page sitting alone with nothing pointing at it.

Google Ads runs alongside it. Exact match only, tightly themed ad groups, rebuilt bidding. Ads cover the searches organic has not reached yet and give an early read on which terms are worth building a page for.

What the bidding rebuild did on Mamba's own Google Ads account. From 20 August to 7 September 2026 the account took 33 clicks. In the 19 days before it, 1 to 19 August 2026, the same account took 19 clicks, and it spent 27% more to get them. Same daily budget in both windows. Two matched 19 day reads, either side of the rebuild. Put together, the cost of a click fell 58%. If you want ads on your own account, message us on WhatsApp and we will talk it through.

How we report

Nothing here is estimated

A scan whose search layer cannot be read off the export does not get published here, whatever it says. If the layer is not printed on screen, the pair cannot be shown to be a like for like read, so it does not go up.
