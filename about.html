How does it work?

It's Pretty Easy.

01.Grid scan of your area

Before anything is promised, I scan your service area as a grid and read your position in every square, not just standing at your own front door. The squares you can realistically win set the order of the work.

02.Google Business Profile

Categories, services, description, photos and posts, set against what the businesses already sitting in the top 3 are filed under. This is the part that moves the map fastest, so it goes first.

03.Website and citations

A page for every service on your profile, filed under the same category, plus a page for each area you actually serve, written with real local detail instead of the city name swapped in. Then your name, address and phone confirmed across the directories so Google stops seeing three versions of you.

04.The same grid, again

Not a traffic chart. The identical grid re-run on the same keyword, so you watch red squares turn green in the places your customers really search from. You keep running your crews the whole time. You don't get a homework list.

Organic comes first because it keeps working after you stop paying for it. Ads run when there's a reason for them, not by default.
