Why you're losing business every morning

29 April 2025·1 min read

What's the first thing you do in the morning?

And be honest. I know you're not that guy on Instagram who is dumping his face in ice water at 5 am.

Sure. That stuff looks great on Instagram.

And if you do wake up at 3:52 am to dump your head in ice water, great!

But most people, including your customers, wake up, grab their phone and within the first 5 minutes of opening their eyes... they're scrolling on some sort of social media.

Probably not good for your brain to get artificial light first thing, but that's the way the world goes. Which means you can adapt and use it as an opportunity.

Most of your potential customers are checking their socials immediately when they wake up. And they check at lunch time, and they check after dinner, and they're scrolling before they sleep.

That's why you have to be there. Because if you won't be there for them, your competition will be.

YOU want to be seen as the expert in your local area, not the competition. Which means they need to see YOUR content and paid ads to be reminded of what you do.

And it has never been easier to reach the right clients. Meta makes it easy. Google makes it easy.

So ask yourself: 'are my clients seeing my stuff?'

If yes -> great. Double down on it. It's worth it.

If no -> make 2025 the year that clients can't ignore you. Even if they try.
