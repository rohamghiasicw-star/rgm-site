Why Good Writing = More Sales for Your Business

9 February 2025·2 min read

Good writing is the best way to create content, attract attention and get clients to grow your business.

Being a good writer is a superpower.

But there’s one problem.

Most writing is boring, putting your to sleep or it's someone waffling.

And it seems like whichever language or country you’re in, it’s the same case.

Why?

Probably because school never taught us how to write interesting stuff.

And people haven’t picked up on the writing secrets that puts the spice into whatever you’re writing.

The Writing Secret That Hooks Readers Instantly

The first step is to seduce people to read what you wrote.

You could write out the cure to cancer but if you can't get people to read far enough...they'll never find out what you have to say.

Let’s do a thought experiment:

How many people really read newspapers anymore?

Not a lot, right?

It’s because you take one look at it, you see the dense formatted lines, the long paragraphs and your brain says ‘There’s no way we’re reading all that’.

Compare that to the article you’re reading right now…It’s a lot more digestible.

I don’t use technical jargon or do any run-on sentences. I use short paragraphs. Easy to understand sentences.

Short Form vs Long Form Content

So does that mean you should make everything easy? Chop it up like babyfood? Do a sentence per line just so it looks visually pleasing?

Nope.

But you do need to be mindful of how you format your stuff. Doesn't matter if it's a presentation, audio or a post.

Any type of content you make needs to hook the reader to consume it.

That’s just the start though of writing for more sales. Much more coming.
