How Does It Work?

It's Pretty easy.

01.Grid scan of your area

Before anything is promised, we scan your service area as a grid and read your position in every square, not just standing at your own front door. The squares you can realistically win set the order of the work.

02.Google Business Profile

Categories, services, description, photos and posts, set against what the businesses already sitting in the top 3 are filed under. This is the part that moves the map fastest, so it goes first.

03.Website and citations

A page for every service on your profile, filed under the same category, plus a page for each area you actually serve. Then your name, address and phone confirmed across the directories so Google stops seeing three versions of you.

04.The same grid, again

Not a traffic chart. The identical grid re-run on the same keyword, so you can see which squares turned green and where the calls are coming from now.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Ads put you at the top for as long as you keep paying for every click. SEO puts you in the top 3 of the map and the clicks stop costing you. We run both. For most local businesses the organic work is the one that keeps paying after you stop.

Which companies do you work with?

Local service businesses. Right now that is movers, painters, drainage and cabinet refinishing, across British Columbia, Alberta and Ontario. If people find you by searching a service plus a city, the system fits.

How much time will I need to invest?

Almost none. One onboarding where we get access to your Google Business Profile and the basics about your business. After that we handle it. You keep running the business and answering the phone.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. The work runs month to month, so you can leave whenever it stops being worth it. Everything gets built on your own Google Business Profile and your own website, so the profile, the pages and the citations stay with you either way.
